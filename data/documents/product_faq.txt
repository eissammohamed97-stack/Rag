Product FAQ

What is the return policy?
Customers may return any unused product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Items must be in their original packaging with all tags attached.
Return shipping is free for defective items, but customers cover the cost for other returns.

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days within the country.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs an additional $15.
International shipping typically takes 10 to 20 business days depending on the destination.

Do you offer a warranty?
All electronic products come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or unauthorized repairs.
Extended warranty plans of up to 3 years can be purchased at checkout.

How do I contact customer support?
Customer support is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, via live chat and email.
The average response time for email support is under 24 hours.
Phone support is available for Premium subscribers only.
