New Employee Onboarding Guide

Week 1: Setup and Orientation
New employees receive their laptop and login credentials on their first day.
IT setup, including email, VPN access, and internal tools, is completed during the first two days.
A welcome session with HR covers benefits enrollment, payroll setup, and company policies.
Each new employee is assigned a "buddy" from their team to help them settle in.

Week 2: Role-Specific Training
Employees begin shadowing team members relevant to their role.
Engineering hires complete a codebase walkthrough and set up their local development environment.
Sales hires complete product training and shadow at least two live customer calls.

30-60-90 Day Plan
By day 30, employees should understand team goals and complete initial training modules.
By day 60, employees are expected to contribute independently to at least one active project.
By day 90, a formal check-in with the manager reviews performance and sets goals for the next quarter.

Benefits Enrollment
Health insurance enrollment must be completed within the first 30 days of employment.
The company matches 401(k) contributions up to 4% of the employee's salary.
Employees become eligible for the annual bonus program after 90 days of employment.
