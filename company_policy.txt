Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval.
Full-time remote work requires written approval from both the direct manager and HR.
All remote employees must be reachable during core hours, from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.
Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a stable internet connection and a quiet workspace.
The company provides a one-time home office stipend of $500 for new remote employees.
Equipment issued by the company (laptop, monitor, headset) remains company property and must be
returned upon termination of employment.

Vacation Policy

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year during their first two years.
After two years of continuous employment, the accrual rate increases to 20 days per year.
Unused vacation days may be carried over into the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.
Vacation requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance for approval.

Expense Reimbursement

Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring the expense.
All expenses over $75 require an itemized receipt.
Travel expenses, including flights and hotels, must be pre-approved by a manager before booking.
Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of an approved expense report.
